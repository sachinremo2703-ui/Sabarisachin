HVAC workflow

  1. Linked the Revit architectural model into the MEP system template.
  2. Bound the link so I could add floor and ceiling plans to the mechanical project.
  3. Created spaces and space tags, then ran heating and cooling calculations for each space.
  4. Generated schedules and quantities.
  5. Placed diffusers on every ceiling, added VAV boxes and routed supply and return ducts from them.
  6. Matched the calculated CFM of each room with its diffusers to avoid uneven air supply.
  7. Connected diffusers with flexible duct and ran duct sizing. Supply velocity 1500 to 2000 fpm, return 1000 to 1500 fpm.
  8. Checked clashes with structure (beams, slabs, walls), electrical (cable trays, panels) and plumbing (pipes, tanks), using section views, and coordinated fixes.

Electrical workflow

  1. Built lux and lumens schedules from each room's height and width and its lighting fixtures.
  2. Placed single, three-way and four-way switches and connected them to the fixtures.
  3. Allocated sub panels for the light and switch circuits and connected them to the main panel.
  4. Created arc wiring diagrams for every ceiling to show the circuit lines between lights, switches and panels.
  5. Used cable trays on the 5th floor to cut maintenance cost, and conduits for power between panels, lights and switches.
  6. Checked clashes with ducts and pipes in section and 3D views.
